02 / The origin story

Failure became a risk culture

A mortgage bet broke when borrowers repaid early. The lesson was not “predict perfectly.” It was “model cash-flow behavior better.”

1986

Fink’s First Boston team loses about $100m after misreading mortgage risk.

1988

Eight founders start the business that becomes BlackRock.

2008

Crisis-era mandates demonstrate institutional trust in its analytics.

2009

BlackRock acquires BGI, including iShares. The video incorrectly says 2008.

2026

AUM reaches $15.3tn; iShares crosses $6tn.
